Privacy policy

Lightkeepers is a puzzle game about bending lighthouse beams to send lost glimmers home. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. In short: the current version does not collect any personal data.

What the game stores

  • Your progress (stars per level, Endless Sea depth), your sound and music settings and your remaining hints are saved only on your device.
  • This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Future features

Online races and advertising are planned for later versions. Before they are switched on, this page will be updated to explain exactly what they use (for example an anonymous player ID and the display name you choose for leaderboards, and the data an ad network needs to show ads), and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, including in the European Union.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and have it deleted. Because the current version holds none on our side, there is nothing to export or delete; your on-device data is removed when you delete the app. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug, or have an idea for the game? Write to us and include your device model and the level number if it is about a puzzle.

bayoudhibilel@gmail.com

Common questions

  • How do I win a level? Every glimmer must be lit in its own colour at the same moment. Fewer moves earn more stars.
  • I'm stuck. Tap the bulb button at the top of the screen for a hint. It shows the next tile and where to place it.
  • Can I replay the tutorial? Yes: Settings (gear on the home screen) then "How to play".
  • Where are online races? They are coming in a future update.

We usually reply within a few days.
