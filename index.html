Privacy policy

Lightkeepers is a puzzle game about bending lighthouse beams to send lost glimmers home. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

What the game stores

  • Your progress (stars per level, Endless Sea depth), your sound and music settings and your remaining hints are saved only on your device.
  • This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics of our own, no location, no contacts, camera or microphone.
  • On iPhone the game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps (no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • We never sell your data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The game shows a full-screen ad now and then between levels, and an optional video you can choose to watch for a free hint. Ads are limited to family-safe content.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure results, Google's SDK collects data such as your device's IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app identifiers, device type and operating system, and how you interact with the ads. This data goes directly to Google, not to us.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form the first time it starts. You can accept, refuse, or choose options. Refusing still lets you play; you then see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices (ads)".
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Future features

Online races are planned for a later version. Before they are switched on, this page will be updated to explain exactly what they use (for example an anonymous player ID and the display name you choose for leaderboards).

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and have it deleted. We hold none on our side, so there is nothing to export or delete; your on-device data is removed when you delete the app. For ad data, change your consent in Settings → "Privacy choices (ads)", or contact Google through the links above. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Common questions

  • How do I win a level? Every glimmer must be lit in its own colour at the same moment. Fewer moves earn more stars.
  • I'm stuck. Tap the bulb button at the top of the screen for a hint. It shows the next tile and where to place it.
  • Can I replay the tutorial? Yes: Settings (gear on the home screen) then "How to play".
  • Where are online races? They are coming in a future update.
  • Can I change my ad consent? Yes: Settings, then "Privacy choices (ads)" (shown where consent applies, such as the EU).

We usually reply within a few days.
